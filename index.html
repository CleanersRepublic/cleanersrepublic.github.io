Why we exist

Cleaners keep 100%.
Always.

Most cleaning platforms take 20–30% out of every job a cleaner does. We think that's backwards — the person doing the work should be the one who benefits most from it.

Cleaners Republic was built in Chelsea on one simple idea: every job is priced fairly by us, and every penny of that price goes to the cleaner. No commission, no hidden cuts, no middleman taking a slice of someone else's hard work.
